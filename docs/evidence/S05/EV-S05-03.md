## Implemented

- Exactly one visible panel may be focused. Opening, restoring or explicitly focusing a
  panel moves it to the newest z-order and records it in deterministic focus history.
- Minimizing or closing the focused panel restores the most recent eligible visible
  panel. Closed/minimized panels cannot retain focus.
- Closing a minimized panel is supported without requiring an active GameObject.
- Pin state is retained across minimize/restore/close transitions; focus/pin visual
  state is resynchronized to every active `PanelView` after accepted commands.
- Duplicate open/focus, focus of a hidden panel and restore of a non-minimized panel fail
  without state/version mutation.
